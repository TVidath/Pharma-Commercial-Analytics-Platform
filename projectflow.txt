================================================================================
  1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Pharma Commercial Analytics Platform is a complete commercial analytics platform built for a
simulated pharmaceutical company operating across India.

Company Profile (Simulated):
  - 50 sales representatives
  - 5 regional managers
  - 1,500 doctors
  - 150 hospitals
  - 5 product brands across 2 therapeutic areas (Cardiology & Diabetology)
  - Pan-India operations (North & South zones)

Problem: Stagnant revenue despite rising promotional spend.
Root Cause: Allocation and effectiveness problem, not demand.

Hypothesis:
  A large share of effort is concentrated on saturated high-volume prescribers
  and mature products, while a quantifiable pool of growth-elastic doctors,
  hospitals and territories is under-served. Reallocating 15-25% of selling
  capacity and re-weighting marketing can lift revenue ~8-12% in 12-18 months
  at FLAT COST.

What I Built:
  - Synthetic data engine generating ~500,000 rows with business patterns
  - Full ETL pipeline: generation -> cleaning -> validation -> PostgreSQL load
  - Dimensional data model (star schema) with SQL KPI library
  - Doctor segmentation (Potential x Value 9-box) and hospital tiering
  - Performance diagnostics (Rx, regional, product, marketing)
  - Revenue forecasting (ETS model + intervention overlay)
  - Commercial Opportunity Score (0-100 doctor prioritisation index)
  - Territory optimization and capacity-neutral call reallocation
  - 7-page interactive Streamlit executive dashboard
  - Automated end-to-end via Makefile with pytest tests


================================================================================
  2. SIX BUSINESS PATTERNS IN THE SIMULATION
================================================================================

  1. Sales effort tracks CURRENT Rx (corr=0.72), not POTENTIAL (corr=0.28)
     -> Effort is mis-allocated; high-potential doctors are under-served.

  2. Diminishing returns on call frequency
     -> Saturation curve: r = 1 - exp(-0.35 * calls)

  3. 1 under-invested territory has disproportionate white space

  4. A declining hero brand ("Lipivas") masks portfolio growth
     -> Growth brands are hidden by the decliner.

  5. Marketing spend is skewed toward a low-ROI channel
     -> Print & Conferences: 34% budget, low ROI
     -> Digital & CME: small budget, high ROI

  6. Specific zones are losing competitive share

================================================================================
  6. EXECUTION PIPELINE — END-TO-END FLOW (9 PHASES)
================================================================================

------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PHASE 1: Data Generation
  Command: make generate -> python scripts/generate_data.py
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Step 1: load_config() -> reads engagement_config.yaml

  Step 2: build_dimensions(cfg)
    File: src/catalyst/data_generation/dimensions.py
    Creates 12 dimension tables:
      dim_month, dim_zone, dim_region, dim_regional_manager,
      dim_territory, dim_sales_rep, dim_hospital, dim_doctor,
      dim_therapeutic_area, dim_product, dim_competitor_product, dim_campaign

  Step 3: compute_doctor_latents(cfg, dims)
    File: src/catalyst/data_generation/latent.py
    Two hidden drivers per doctor:
      - potential (0-1): structural prescribing capacity
      - adoption (0-1): how much potential is currently realized
    Creates white space by decoupling adoption for:
      - Competitor-loyal doctors (~18%): adoption x 0.45
      - Under-invested territories: adoption x 0.60

  Step 4: build_facts(cfg, dims, latents)
    File: src/catalyst/data_generation/facts.py
    Creates 4 fact tables (~500,000 rows):
      fact_prescriptions, fact_competitor_rx, fact_sales_calls,
      fact_marketing_spend

  Step 5: write_raw(tables) -> 16 CSVs to data/raw/

  OUTPUT: data/raw/*.csv (16 CSV files, ~500k rows total)


------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PHASE 2: Data Pipeline — Cleaning & Validation
  Command: make validate -> python scripts/validate_data.py
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Step 1: clean_all() -> enforce dtypes, remove negatives, dedup
    File: src/catalyst/data_pipeline/clean.py
    Writes cleaned CSVs to data/processed/

  Step 2: load_tables() + run_all() -> 3 categories of validation:
    a) Structural: FK integrity, grain uniqueness, NOT NULL, ranges
    b) Plausibility: revenue reconciliation, seasonality
    c) Business-pattern recovery: validates embedded patterns

  Step 3: write_report() -> reports/data_quality_report.md

  OUTPUT: data/processed/*.csv + reports/data_quality_report.md


------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PHASE 3: Database Build — PostgreSQL Load
  Command: make db-up -> make db-load
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. docker compose up -d -> PostgreSQL 15 container (port 5432)
  2. Execute sql/schema/01_create_tables.sql (16 tables)
  3. Bulk COPY all 16 CSVs (single transaction)
  4. Execute sql/schema/02_indexes_constraints.sql
  5. Build views (sql/views/01_kpi_views.sql)
  6. Build stored procedures (sql/procedures/01_procedures.sql)
  7. ANALYZE on all fact tables

  OUTPUT: Fully loaded PostgreSQL database


------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PHASE 4: Diagnostic Analytics — EDA
  Command: make eda -> python scripts/run_eda.py
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Generates 10 diagnostic charts + reports/eda_report.md:
    01-10: Revenue trends, zone distribution, product portfolio,
           doctor ranking, call heatmap, Pareto, marketing spend,
           seasonality, competitive share

  SQL Analysis Library (sql/analysis/):
    01_ranking_and_targeting.sql (doctor ranking, NTILE)
    02_regional_and_growth.sql (growth, YoY, ROLLUP)
    03_product_and_marketing.sql (product P&L, channel ROI)
    04_salesforce_effectiveness.sql (rep productivity)

  OUTPUT: reports/eda_report.md + reports/figures/01_* through 10_*


------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PHASE 5: Customer Segmentation
  Command: make segment -> python scripts/run_segmentation.py
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Doctor Segmentation (src/catalyst/segmentation/doctors.py):
    Transparent potential score (0-100):
      0.40 x panel + 0.20 x specialty + 0.20 x hospital
    + 0.10 x urbanicity + 0.10 x KOL

    Potential x Value 9-Box:
      Champions (H,H), Rising Stars (H,M), Hidden Gems (H,L)
      Reliable Core (M,H), Steady (M,M), Developers (M,L)
      Loyal Small (L,H), Occasional (L,M), Low Priority (L,L)
    Each segment: action, cadence, channels, KPI

    K-Means validation (k=4): ARI + Silhouette Score

  Hospital Segmentation (src/catalyst/segmentation/hospitals.py):
    Tiers: Platinum (top 10%) > Gold > Silver > Bronze (bottom 30%)

  OUTPUT: reports/segmentation_report.md
          data/processed/doctor_segments.csv
          data/processed/hospital_segments.csv


------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PHASE 6: Performance & Opportunity Diagnostics
  Command: make performance -> python scripts/run_performance.py
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Prescription opportunity (doctor-level Rx gaps, white space Rs)
  Regional & market performance (TTM revenue, growth, share)
  Product portfolio (growth vs margin, lifecycle stages)
  Marketing effectiveness (channel ROI, budget share)

  Opportunity Register (Rs Cr):
    White space + Share recapture + Marketing reallocation

  OUTPUT: reports/performance_report.md
          data/processed/opportunity_register.csv


------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PHASE 7: Forecasting & Opportunity Scoring
  Command: make score -> python scripts/run_forecast_scoring.py
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Revenue Forecast (src/catalyst/forecasting/forecast.py):
    ETS model (additive trend + seasonal, 12-period)
    Baseline + intervention overlay (phased opportunity capture)

  Opportunity Score (src/catalyst/scoring/opportunity_score.py):
    Score = 0.30 x Potential + 0.30 x Headroom + 0.15 x Momentum
          + 0.15 x Accessibility + 0.10 x Competitive
    Priority: P1 (top 20%) | P2 (50-80%) | P3 (bottom 50%)

  OUTPUT: reports/forecast_and_scoring_report.md
          data/processed/doctor_opportunity_score.csv
          data/processed/territory_opportunity_score.csv


------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PHASE 8: Territory & Sales-Force Optimization
  Command: make optimize -> python scripts/run_optimization.py
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Ideal call plan: P1=18/yr, P2=9/yr, P3=3/yr

  Capacity-neutral reallocation:
    rec_calls = current_capacity x (ideal / total_ideal)
    ~34% of calls shift from P3 -> P1 at FLAT COST

  Territory actions:
    Deploy rep (vacant) | Reinforce (under-served)
    Over-served (trim) | Balanced

  OUTPUT: reports/optimization_report.md
          data/processed/doctor_call_plan.csv
          data/processed/territory_plan.csv


------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PHASE 9: Executive Dashboard
  Command: make dashboard
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Step 1: prepare_dashboard.py
    Precompute 8 aggregate CSVs for instant loading

  Step 2: streamlit run dashboard/streamlit/app.py
    7 interactive pages (NO database required):
      1. Executive Overview
      2. Sales Performance
      3. Regional Performance
      4. Doctor Segmentation
      5. Hospital Analysis
      6. Marketing ROI
      7. Product Analysis

  OUTPUT: Running Streamlit dashboard at localhost
